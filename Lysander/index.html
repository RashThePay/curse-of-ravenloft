Dissonant Whispers can force movement and provoke allies’ opportunity attacks. Heroism stabilizes a frontliner.

Rest checklist

Song of Rest adds 1d6 healing if a Hit Die was spent. Bardic Inspiration and Tales reset on a short rest; spell slots on a long rest.
