The rules that matter

Features

Reckless Attack BARBARIAN 2

On your first attack of the turn, choose to gain advantage on Strength-based melee attacks this turn. Attacks against you have advantage until your next turn.

Frenzy BERSERKER 3

When entering Rage, choose to frenzy. After that turn, make one melee weapon attack as a bonus action each turn. When Rage ends, gain one level of exhaustion.

Unarmored Defense AC 14

While not wearing armor, AC equals 10 + DEX (+1) + CON (+3). You may still use a shield.

Savage Attacks HALF-ORC

On a melee-weapon critical hit, roll one of the weapon’s damage dice one additional time and add it to the critical damage.

Darkvision & Menacing HALF-ORC

See in darkness within 60 ft as dim light, in shades of gray. Menacing grants proficiency in Intimidation.

Heart of Darkness HAUNTED ONE

Commoners recognize the darkness you carry and usually offer aid and shelter, unless you have shown yourself to be dangerous to them.
