The company is 4th level.

Nobody gets a new subclass trick at this step. Everyone gets tougher, and everyone has to choose how they grow. The sheets stay at their current numbers until those choices are in.

Automatic · no player input

What changes for everyone

  • Level 4 on the folio. Proficiency bonus stays +2.
  • One more Hit Die of their class (Desmond and the casters: d8; Thud: d12).
  • Hit points. This table has used the average so far, so the default is the average again. A player can still ask to roll instead.
  • No Extra Attack, no new Sneak Attack die, no extra Rage, no bigger Bardic Inspiration die, no new invocation, no proficiency bump. Those wait for later levels.
Every player decides

Ability Score Improvement or a feat

Take one of these, not both:

  • +2 to one ability score, or +1 to two different scores. Cap is 20.
  • One feat, if the table allows feats. A feat that raises an ability still counts as the whole choice.

If Constitution goes up enough to raise the modifier, maximum and current HP also go up by 1 per character level (so +4 right now), on top of the level-4 hit points.

Staying in the current class is the expected path. Multiclassing instead of taking 4th level in that class is a table decision, and it would skip this ASI.

  • Demian · d8 + 0 CON → +5 · 18 → 23

Player decides

Three required picks

  • ASI or feat. The cleanest ASI is Charisma 18 and Constitution 12. That is +4 Bardic Inspiration uses, DC 14, spell attack +6, Healing Word and Heroism both scale with the new modifier, and +4 HP from the Constitution change (27 total if the average was used). +2 Charisma also works if he wants to leave Constitution odd. War Caster or Resilient (Constitution) are the usual feat answers to Barovia concentration checks.
  • One new bard cantrip. Guidance already does not count. Strong fits: Minor Illusion, Mage Hand, Prestidigitation, Message, or Light.
  • One new bard spell of 1st or 2nd level. He currently knows Disguise Self, Healing Word, Heroism, Dissonant Whispers, Sleep, and Borrowed Knowledge. Useful 2nd-level additions in this campaign: Suggestion, Invisibility, Lesser Restoration, Hold Person, Silence, Enhance Ability, or Calm Emotions.

Optional when he levels: replace one known bard spell with another he can cast. If the table uses Tasha’s, Bardic Versatility can also swap one expertise or the class cantrip (not Guidance).
